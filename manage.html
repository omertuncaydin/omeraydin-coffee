With the CoffeeNutz admin account, the same one as for the other tools.

An open eye means the block shows on omeraydin.coffee, a closed eye hides it. The list follows the page from top to bottom. Nothing changes on the site until you press Publish.

    The learner cards, in their order on the page. Every public course review on coffeenutz.net has a card here; a card you have not chosen yet is hidden. The eye shows or hides a card and, like on the Visibility tab, waits for Publish. Edit text opens the headline, name, title, company and quote of a card; the arrows move a card up or down. A logo you choose is shown next to the name, and Logo on / Logo off shows or hides it. Text, order and logo are saved at once. For the photo: choose a picture, drag it inside the frame until the crop is right, then save; it is made smaller before it is sent. Download saves the picture of a card, or its logo, to your computer.

    The texts of the pop-up that “More about Ömer Aydın” opens. Change a text and press Save; visitors see it the next time they open the site. A link is written as [words](https://address), bold as **words**. In the list every line is one entry. A text you empty is left out of the pop-up.

    The block “Original Research & Methodology” on the site: its texts, the pop-ups behind its steps, the pop-up “Further research” under them, and the recording that its play button plays. Change a text and press Save; visitors see it the next time they open the site. Bold is written as **words**, a link as [words](https://address). In the steps every line is one step. A text you empty is left out of the block; the label, the title and the two buttons cannot be empty.

    Each of the first four steps opens a pop-up. Its headline, its text and the line under its figure come as first drafts: change them under “Pop-up behind step …” below. A step whose pop-up has neither a headline nor a text is a plain name on the site, without its round button. Under the steps the block has one more entry, outside their numbering, with a pop-up of its own: “Further research”, the last group below. Its name cannot be empty; while its pop-up has neither a headline nor a text, the entry is not shown. Where a pop-up has a place for a picture, its group shows it: the picture that comes with the site files, until you choose another one or “Show no picture”; a place without any picture is left out. Download saves the picture a place shows to your computer. The eye for the whole block and one for each pop-up are on the Visibility tab.
